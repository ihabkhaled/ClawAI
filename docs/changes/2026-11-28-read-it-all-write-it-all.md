## Before

A file larger than the model's window was answered from its opening and a few matching passages; an answer cut by the
output limit stayed cut; Threads refused topics over 10,000 characters.

## Change

Oversized files are read in model-sized parts (notes, or the request carried out on every part), cut answers are
continued, Threads accepts and reads topics up to 1,000,000 characters, and the smart attachments page says so.

## Now

Knowledge homes: [ADR-231](../13-adr/adr-231-read-it-all-write-it-all.md),
[rule 57](../../rules/57-memory-and-context-pack-integrity.md), [chat guide](../04-backend/service-guide-chat.md),
[QA record](../qa-evidence/2026-11-28-read-it-all-write-it-all.md).

## Why

Owner order 2026-11-28 after a 42,417-byte brief was cut on a small model.

## Verification

See the QA record.

## What would make this stale

A change to the window fit, the part sizes or caps, the Threads limits, or the narration kinds.
