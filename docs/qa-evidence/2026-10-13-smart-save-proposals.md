## Findings

- The reviewers caught real defects before shipping: a half-saved confirm shown as Saved, dialog limits that did not match the backend, pre-filter cues that matched everyday words (ru, hi, zh, it, th, ja), a record that could stay SAVING forever, an empty card after Not now.
- Live: the planner refused Call me Jonas from here on as not explicit; in ASK mode the user confirms first, so the planner prompt now accepts standing preferences (AUTO keeps the strict rule).
