## Findings

- Notifications showed "Untitled draft" because Threads published before titles were derived keep that placeholder in the revision row. Fixed at the source of the notice: the title is derived from the article when the stored one is the placeholder, and is empty (not the placeholder) when nothing is readable.
- The request notice linked to the public page, where only the requester form exists. It now links to `/threads/review/<id>#change-request-<id>`.
- The owner review list was only rendered for a published Thread and only after the generation section loaded; it now also shows for a Thread with pending requests.
- The public Thread payload never contained change requests (checked, now under test). The only new public read is names of accepted or applied requesters.
- A bare `/login` or `/register` is answered with a redirect to the English screen. About 30 links and 8 redirects in marketing, auth and the Thread and Board pages were bare; all now carry the page language, guarded by `public-auth-links-locale.test.tsx`.
- Found while testing: several existing specs hard-coded bare hrefs and were updated to the localized ones.

## Open gaps

- L03, L04, L05, L06, L07, L08, L09, L12, L13: a live pass against a running stack, closed by the owner or the next QA run before deploy. Deploy order: shared-types and threads-service migration first (adds an enum value and an index), then auth-service and frontend.
- L15: read GitHub CI after the owner commits and pushes.
- Not built: a model that rewrites the article from a suggestion automatically.
