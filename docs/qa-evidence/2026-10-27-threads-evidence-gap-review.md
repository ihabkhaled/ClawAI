- The first replay run showed the existing E1 to E5 scenarios used a blocker that mentions "sources"; with the new loop they would have triggered research. The stub blocker was changed; the new scenarios cover the research path explicitly.
- Evidence ids come from the research service; fetched pages get a short hash of their URL, so a re-found page keeps its id. The merge therefore dedupes by id and by normalised URL and keeps every existing item untouched.
