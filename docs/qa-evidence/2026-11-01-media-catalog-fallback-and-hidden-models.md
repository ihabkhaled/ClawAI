## Findings

From production QA of v1.226.0: the fallback chain used fixed model ids absent on the production
accounts; Veo (3), chatgpt-image-latest and two dated mini-tts rows were hidden because their
connector rows were lifecycle REMOVED (provider list omits them); audio phrasing without the word
'audio' as a noun was not offered the tool; TTS/Lyria showed a Vision badge. All fixed here.
