## Findings

- Classifier prompt v1 misread music requests and pasted briefs on gpt-oss:120b; prompt tightened 3 times and the classifier now prefers gemma4:31b within the published chain (27/27 on every run).
- Compare/Consensus/Escalation/labs do not receive message.routed, so they keep keyword rules (documented in service-guide-chat).
- Deploy order: thread-generation-service must deploy with or before chat-service (new optional snapshot fields fail the old integrity check).
