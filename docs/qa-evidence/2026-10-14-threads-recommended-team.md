  preflight refuses with FALLBACK_SAME_PROVIDER. The wizard now replaces backups per seat after
  building the request. The utility and its test are owned by another change and were not edited.
- The shared chat ModelPicker has no disabled option state, so the reason is appended to the label
  and a click is ignored.
