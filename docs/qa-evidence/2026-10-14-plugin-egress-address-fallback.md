## Findings

Cause: the transport pinned the socket to ONE address (first IPv4), so a dead first A record timed out the whole call. Fix: pin to every validated address and let the socket fall back (autoSelectFamily). Not reproducible locally against the real prod network; fix proven with a real socket and unit specs.

L03, L05, L08: closed by re-probing web-fetch of https://example.com on prod after the release.
