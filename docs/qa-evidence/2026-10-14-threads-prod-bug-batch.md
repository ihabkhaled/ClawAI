## Findings

1. Safety scan: fixed. 16-digit ids in citation URLs no longer trip it; a blocked draft is editable (DRAFT with a stored revision), re-scanned, and the owner sees masked excerpts.
2. Topic minimum: fixed, backend and frontend (zh, ja, ko, th, ar, fa, emoji-only, punctuation-only rejected; 4 CJK characters accepted).
3. Slugs: fixed for Hindi, Thai, Tamil, Bengali, Arabic harakat, Persian, Hebrew niqqud; the old frontend validator capped at 64 while the service issues up to 69, now 96.
4. Recommended team: DEGRADED demotion and health ordering added; non-chat and removed rows are dropped. The connector sync that labels `grok-imagine-*` as CHAT is not fixed (follow-up).
5. Distinct authors under fallback: fixed in `RoleHealer`; the step log already shows the real model.
6. Raw markers: numbered or removed at the public read and defensively in the reader (Arabic-Indic digits, CJK brackets, bracketed addresses).
7. Queue: prefetch 3 -> 6, slots 6 -> 12 (new migration), `queue` state in owner progress and UI text in 13 locales. The in-attempt healing waits still hold the slot (reason in ADR-183).
8. Board first page: verified pagination only (`ORDER BY published_at DESC, slug DESC`, no age window; members start language-filtered). No change.
