## Findings

- The backend pages top-level comments by page and pageSize (not by cursor) and sends nextReplyCursor and a removed flag per comment; the frontend follows the real DTO, not the first brief.
- A comment on the URL fragment that is a reply not yet loaded (beyond the first three) is not scrolled to until its replies are loaded.
