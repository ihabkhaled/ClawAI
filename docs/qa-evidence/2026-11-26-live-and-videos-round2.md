## Findings

- YouTube bot-checks data-centre addresses; the device fallback was the "mic on phone" bug. Now server-only with an
  android_vr client retry.
- Several CDNs answer 403 without the page's headers; yt-dlp's are now forwarded (allow-listed).
- RaiNews24 is AES-128 HLS; Steam and TwitCasting serve signed fMP4; webcams have no audio: none can caption.
- TikTok live variants sit on realcrius.com, CBS segments on cbsivideo.com: added to a reviewed pin list.
- The language matrix opened every case at once and hit LIVE_SESSION_LIMIT (the product limit working).
- Production stores an explicit server-pull platform list: the seeder upgrade adds the new platforms.
