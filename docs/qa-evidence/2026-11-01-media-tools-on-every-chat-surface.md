## Findings

Found in local live QA and fixed before this commit: media made by the final step was not linked to
the saved message; Stop on Consensus returned cancelled:false and the image still landed; the lab
surfaces only accept local Ollama models, which get no tools, so they could not make media.

## Open gaps

- Best-of-N, Cost-Ensemble, Role Pack, Pipeline, Decompose, Verify and Repair could not be driven
  live here (they accept only local Ollama models and none is available locally); covered by unit
  specs only. A production or Ollama-enabled run closes this.
- Candidate model calls already in flight when Stop is pressed finish and are discarded.
- L05 e2e spec, L06 Pro and Free check, L08 walk-through: after deploy.
