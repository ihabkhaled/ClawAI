## Findings

1. The first live rounds sent `forcedProvider`/`forcedModel`; the message DTO names them `provider`/`model`, so every "picked" round was really AUTO until fixed. Picked-model rounds were rerun with the right fields.
2. AUTO for Free offered Anthropic and other dear models because the local price table has few rows. Models with no price of their own are now judged by the dearest chat model their provider sells (what auth charges them), which dropped Free's routable set from 333 to 43.
3. `meterUsedPercent` showed 0% after real use (rounding down); anything spent now shows at least 1%.
4. Local DB: the migration was applied by hand to the local auth database; a fresh database gets it from `prisma migrate deploy`.

## Open gaps

L05 committed Playwright spec; L07 device matrix; L13 measurements; L15 GitHub gates. The admin plan form was not walked in the browser. A picked dear model that has no cheaper credit model to fall to and no included model on offer shows the credit refusal (unit-tested, not reproduced live).
