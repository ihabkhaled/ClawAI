## Findings

- Free minutes resolved to 0 for staff accounts (plan slug `admin` missing from the policy). Fixed: policy v2 through the admin API, plus the seed row.
- A history delete racing a run still STOPPING left the run's data orphaned. Fixed: orphan-run sweeper (Redis lock, every 5 min); the DB showed 2 runs tombstoned.
- STT rows had no latency, so the admin caption p95 read "Not measured". Fixed: speech-end→commit latency recorded.
- PDF export answered 501. Fixed: stateless `render-pdf` route in file-generation.
- The PAYG ledger could not charge a policy price. Fixed: fixed-price surface in auth-service.
- Lockfile rewritten by `--legacy-peer-deps` (−3,826 lines). Fixed: regenerated from HEAD (+hls.js, +live-service only).
- `install-tls.ps1` fell back to self-signed openssl and replaced the mkcert CA on this machine. Fixed: re-issued with mkcert (same CA, `live-service` SAN). The script fallback itself is open.
- YouTube: `@handle` links failed with a generic error, `/channel/UC…` links were unsupported, and the side panel promised summaries for a player-only source. Fixed all three, plus a "Watch on YouTube" fallback link and an explicit iframe referrer policy.
- Marketing title read "… | ClawAI | ClawAI". Fixed.
- 10 YouTube channels: Al Jazeera English, DW News, France 24 English, euronews, CNA and Al Arabiya played live in the modal. BBC News, Sky News, NASA and ABC News showed YouTube's "This video is unavailable" (no current live stream on that channel id, or embedding off); the fallback link was present.
