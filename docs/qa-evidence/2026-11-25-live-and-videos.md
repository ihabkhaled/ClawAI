## Findings

- All six owner links were recorded videos; only live links were recognised. Recorded videos are now a capability.
- Facebook share links need one redirect followed, and Facebook answers `/unsupportedbrowser` unless the request names
  a client: sent as `ClawAI-LinkCheck/1.0`.
- Odysee and Weibo failed because the canonical URL escaped `:` and `@`; host-matched paths are no longer escaped.
- A live YouTube news channel failed server pull: its master playlist (12 h rewind) was 1.04 MB against a 1 MB cap.
- Kick past-broadcast links were silently read as the live channel; they are now their own recorded video.
- Summary billing over-counted wall time when no new video was processed; fixed before shipping.

## Open gaps

- L15: GitHub CI is read after the push.
- Vimeo (login wall), Reddit and Douyin (cookies), Naver TV and TwitCasting VODs (yt-dlp errors) fail; not claimed.
- Live-only platforms without a live channel to test today were not exercised live (same yt-dlp path).
- A summary bills and progresses only while the page is open (heartbeats).
