## Findings

Fixed, with tests:

1. Parallel authors lost a call to a Serializable conflict (P2034) shown as PAYG_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE: every budget transaction now retries with jittered backoff; a call that still conflicts is a 503 THREAD_BUDGET_BUSY (temporary), and a spent cap is PAYG_CREDIT_EXHAUSTED carrying what is left.
2. A retried request id got a false "credit exhausted": calls/reserve is idempotent per request id (ALREADY_RESERVED; reopen on a new hold; ALREADY_FINALIZED or ALREADY_RELEASED for a duplicate of a finished hold).
3. Free had no allowance for Threads: THREADS is eligible and its calls are not held to the monthly count while a meter exists.
4. The budget counted holds: the credit finalize settles the call at the charge; the hold is clamped to the cap left (a punitive unpriced-model hold shortens the answer).
5. A cancelled job stayed ACTIVE: close releases in-flight calls and their credit holds in one transaction.
6. A failed enqueue leaked a budget and a Free allowance hold: reserve releases in finally, a lost create race adopts the winner, and a sweeper closes budgets ACTIVE for 12 h and releases stranded allowance holds.

Still open (outside this workspace): thread-generation-service request id lacks the job attempt (generation-pipeline.manager.ts:717; recommended jobId:roleKey:round-N:attempt-JOBATTEMPT:cand-INDEX); it should call close(RELEASED) when an enqueue fails and map NUL or lone-surrogate topics to 400; routing-service still prices an unpriced model at a punitive fallback (511k micro-USD for 7.6k in and 4k out) instead of refusing it; the progress spend should read POST internal/threads/budgets/summary. The shared-entitlements dist must be rebuilt for chat-service and thread-generation-service to use the detailed verdict.
