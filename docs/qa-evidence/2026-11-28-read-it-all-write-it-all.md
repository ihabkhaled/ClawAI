## Findings

Found while testing: half-window parts left no room for a translation, so each part came back cut (3,327 of about
20,000 characters); each-part mode now uses quarter-window parts and continues a cut part. A small model's notes
missed the closing instruction until the reader was told to copy headings, instructions and closing lines word for
word. Test threads and files were throwaway local data.
