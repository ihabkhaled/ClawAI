## Findings

- D-01 retry skipped the preflight: fixed, retry now runs the same checks on the stored seats plus `models[]`.
- D-02 parallel starts: fixed by a per-owner gate, a bounded retry on P2034/P2002 and a threads-side replay by owner and key (4 same-key starts give 1 job).
- D-03 orphan jobs: prevented by a compensating cancel after a failed publication write; existing orphans are repaired through the moderator endpoint.
- D-06 cap: 3 queued or running per owner, 429 with a code.
- D-07 unknown model: exact match against the picker catalog; verified live.
- D-08 global keys: scoped per owner.
- D-09: allowance vs plan split by re-reading the plan; "used N of M" and reset date are not available without an auth-service change.
- D-11 cancel: 409 with `state` unless QUEUED or RUNNING.
- D7 republish: works for an approved unpublished revision. D8 could not be reproduced; a duplicate publish request is the probable cause and is now idempotent.
- D-12 and D-04 input: topic trimmed and checked, NUL and lone surrogates refused with codes.
- Found while working: the generation service does not release a budget when `createQueued` returns STORAGE_ERROR (its `requireStoredJob` throws without releasing). Not changed (other service).
- A second agent was editing apps/claw-threads-service (board search) at the same time; `prisma/schema.prisma`, `CLAUDE.md`, `publications.module.ts`-adjacent files carry both sets of changes.
