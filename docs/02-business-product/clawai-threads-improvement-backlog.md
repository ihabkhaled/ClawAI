## Crystal clear UX checklist

A reviewer ticks every line on the deployed build, in English, Arabic (RTL) and one long-string language (German or Russian), at 320 px and desktop.

1. The Create dialog shows at most four decisions on first view: topic, type, language, and a named "Recommended team"; models sit under Advanced.
2. Before Start the user sees: allowance left and reset date, estimated time range, estimated cost, and the cap (pre-filled).
3. Every model in the picker either works or is badged with a reason and disabled; none known to fail.
4. Start refusals name the cause and give one next step (plans link, wait date, change model); never a generic 403.
5. After Start the first progress appears in 3 s or less; queued jobs show their position.
6. Progress says what is happening now, step x of N, elapsed time, ETA, and "you can leave, we will notify you".
7. Status words are honest: RETRYING shows the reason and attempt; QUEUED never hides a failure.
8. A failure shows one plain sentence and one primary button; codes, role ids and HTTP status sit under Details.
9. Cost is shown as estimate, then spent versus cap; never 0 after a model call; a pre-flight failure says "nothing charged".
10. Cancel responds immediately, completes in 10 s or less, and is not offered where it cannot apply.
11. The draft screen shows who wrote it, Judge/Critic scores and findings in the draft's language, and offers section-level fixes.
12. Publishing shows a preview of the public page, SEO fields and a citation check before the final click.
13. After publishing the user sees share options and what happens next.
14. Keyboard-only path works end to end; focus is visible; live regions announce each step once; contrast passes; no colour-only status.
15. No raw i18n keys in any of 13 locales; ar and fa render RTL without overflow.
16. Works on a phone at 320 px with no horizontal scroll and 44 px touch targets; survives tab hidden, offline and session expiry.

## Definition of release-ready

The build to deploy meets all of these, recorded in docs/qa-evidence (15 lanes, rule 60) and CI green.

Reliability

1. P0-1 deployed and the prod matrix re-run; at least Gemini, Grok, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama-cloud and NVIDIA each complete a job as author, judge and critic, or the provider is hidden in the picker.
2. Default preset: 9 of 10 sequential starts succeed per plan for English; at least 90% per locale on 10 jobs each across the 13 locales (ASSUMPTION, owner to confirm threshold).
3. All 4 publication types complete on prod; fenced JSON, prose-wrapped JSON and Arabic fixtures parse.
4. Deterministic failures end in 10 s or less after one attempt; transient errors retry at most 3 times.
5. No FAILED job with zero step rows; the RESEARCH failure has a documented root cause.

Money and plans 6. spentMicroCredits is non-zero and reconciles with step tokens for at least one job per provider; never above the cap; no credit hold older than 20 min after 100 jobs including cancelled and killed ones. 7. Free users never meet a silent 403; allowance and reset are visible before Start; the allowance rule for failed jobs is decided, documented and tested. 8. Spec, architecture and flagship docs agree with the code (rule 63).

Security and resilience 9. Route-guard table passes on every owner route (owner 2xx, other 404, anonymous 401); plan check holds on retry and fallbacks; no 5xx in a 1,000-request fuzz run (ASSUMPTION). 10. Same-key start is idempotent under 10 parallel requests; replica-restart and redelivery cases produce no duplicate spend. 11. Mid-call cancel reaches CANCELLED in 5 s or less at p95.
