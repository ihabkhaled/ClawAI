# Localization, SEO and Discovery

ClawAI currently generates **13 locales** with roughly **5,199 localization keys**.

The frontend also contains public discovery surfaces such as robots, sitemaps, feeds/RSS, `llms.txt`, manifests, metadata images and localized marketing/public pages.
